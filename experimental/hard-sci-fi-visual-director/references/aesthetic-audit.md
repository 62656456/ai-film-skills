# Visible-image aesthetic audit

Use this audit on the generated image, not on the prompt. Prompt compliance is not image quality.

## Status vocabulary

- **Structure ready**: prompt and references pass internal gates; no image judgment yet.
- **Generated**: an image file exists; quality is unknown.
- **Internal pass**: the image passes this audit; the user has not accepted it.
- **Delivered for review**: the user can see and judge the image.
- **User accepted**: the user explicitly approves or adopts it.
- **Failed / revise**: one or more vetoes or core criteria fail.

Never upgrade internal pass or file existence to user accepted.

## Vetoes

Any one of these blocks a pass regardless of total score:

1. The image fails its selected function-specific visual contract: action is unreadable, discovery lacks observer/evidence/attention shift, relationship lacks spatial or gestural change, establishing lacks world function/active condition, aftermath lacks trace/new state, or an asset reference lacks identity/function/scale/applicable interfaces or relationships. A narrative frame is only a showroom pose or wallpaper.
2. A critical environment error: atmospheric fog in vacuum, wrong gravity direction, Earth weather on an incompatible world, impossible open flame, or unsupported dust/smoke behavior.
3. For photographic, miniature, or sensor-capture routes, no plausible capture location or contradictory perspective; for illustration, animation, or reference routes, contradictory projection, viewpoint, scale, or spatial construction.
4. For a photographed live-action/hybrid route, the frame has no credible source-required photographed anchor or coherent optical integration and instead reads as a clean CG render; practical contact is required only when the beat contains contact. For another route, the image contradicts its declared medium or production logic.
5. Important light or color has no source; multiple incompatible rim lights or universal teal-orange grade dominate.
6. Functional conflict among thrusters, radiators, arrays, docking ports, people, and access paths.
7. People float, brace, walk, use tools, or handle mass in a way that contradicts gravity and inertia.
8. Random glowing seams, decorative holograms, cloned people, plastic skin, or uniform greeble becomes the main evidence of science fiction.
9. A giant machine or organism has no believable support/contact system, mass transfer, motion limit, environmental response, or three independent scale proofs.
10. A giant mecha has no readable source-applicable power/heat and operating, command, automation, or occupied-body relation, or a giant organism has no readable source-applicable support, exchange, energy/homeostasis, or habitat logic; spectacle replaces the system.
11. A weapon/defense/rescue-hazard family is a contemporary gun/tank/missile silhouette with cosmetic panels, or lacks the source-applicable fields needed to explain its effect, responsibility, energy/load, lifecycle, safety, body/agency, or environmental relation. Ordinary civilian equipment is judged by its actual function and must not be forced into weapon criteria.
12. A machine, creature, cockpit, weapon, armor, ship, color identity, or confrontation composition is recognizably copied from an existing franchise.
13. A location is generic wallpaper because the source-required function, spatial relation, environment, active life or causal emptiness, history, institution, scarcity, or abstract/natural condition that should distinguish it is absent. Do not require all categories or invent operations/institutions for natural, empty, ceremonial, abstract, or asset-only spaces.
14. Style or color is carried by a route name, director/film reference, universal palette, equal-weight style mixture, global grade, or state color disconnected from source, medium, material, exposure, and the applicable body/system/world action or state.
15. In related multi-image work, topology, axis, light direction, color semantics, material history, equipment-family grammar, damage, deployment, or story state resets without a declared transition or scene-bible revision.
16. The selected imagination amplitude is II–III, but after removing source names, UI, grade, and setting labels the image can still be replaced by present-day industrial footage or a familiar animal without breaking the source beat; or the selected speculative rule does not create at least three relevant visible consequences in the frame/world design.
17. With the title, prompt, captions, UI text, numbers, and symbols hidden, the image fails the selected function-specific evidence and information-order contract at the viewing time intended by the screenplay. The three-second action chain applies only to beats designed for immediate causal comprehension.
18. In a narrative/world frame, a critical state exists only on a screen or only in explanatory text; the physical mechanism, flow, material/light change, person, institution, or environmental consequence supplies no second evidence. In an explicitly isolated UI/HMI asset, the declared system/task model has no independent internal redundant cue beyond color or tiny text.
19. A story-relevant interface is an isolated black rectangle, bare percentage, random code, seven-segment display, accidental retro panel, decorative grid, or tiny generated text that does not map to the declared task/system and agent or governance relation.
20. The key interface contradicts its source-derived body/sense/task/authority chain, real orientation/node/flow/timing, lacks modality-appropriate acknowledgment and independent system evidence for an irreversible action, or could move unchanged to another scene without losing meaning.
21. A story-important nonhuman organism or ecology is presented as science-derived when it is only hypothetical, or its identity depends on a familiar Earth-animal silhouette, engineered panel shell, decorative blue/cyan emission, LED-like dots, tentacles, translucency, or equal-detail surface with no habitat, energy, sensing, movement/growth, material, or ecological cause.

## Eight-axis score

Score each axis 0, 1, or 2 against the selected source function, production route, deliverable, and imagination amplitude. Inapplicable spectacle, scale, personnel, service, failure, interface, or weapon fields are not missing evidence. Minimum internal candidate threshold: **14/16 with no veto**.

### 1. Dramatic function and visual reading

- 0: the selected action, discovery, relationship, establishing, aftermath, or asset-reference contract is absent or contradicted.
- 1: the intended function exists but one essential evidence link, information step, or emotional/spatial relation is weak.
- 2: the source beat's function, intended information order, and emotional or physical change are clearly expressed without explanatory text.

Score this axis with all UI text mentally hidden. A readable number or caption cannot raise an otherwise unclear beat.

### 2. Hard-science causality

- 0: major physics error or pure decoration.
- 1: environment is broadly plausible but causality is generic or partly invisible.
- 2: every important physical claim fits the stated medium, gravity, energy, heat, load, biology, and motion that apply, with enough visible causal evidence for the selected function. A quiet observation or neutral asset need not invent a second event.

### 3. Production-route coherence

- 0: the image contradicts or cannot reveal its selected production route.
- 1: the route is broadly legible but contact, layering, scale, material, optical/stylistic integration, or extension is weak.
- 2: practical/extension layers form one photographed reality for live action, or line/value/material/space form one deliberate and source-appropriate system for animation, illustration, miniature, or asset reference.

### 4. Viewpoint, projection, and optics

- 0: contradictory viewpoint, projection, perspective, scale relation, or viewer access for the selected medium.
- 1: coherent viewpoint and projection but generic, informationally weak, or emotionally remote.
- 2: the selected route's viewer, camera, or sensor relation—plus lens, depth, exposure, and obstruction where photographic optics apply—places the audience in the exact perceptual relation required by the beat.

### 5. Motivated light and color

- 0: unmotivated multi-color lighting, artificial HDR, or template grade.
- 1: source is plausible but too polished, flat, or decoratively colored.
- 2: source, shadow, bounce, black level, highlight, color causality, and story role are coherent; restraint or intensity matches the screenplay rather than a house look.

### 6. Functional production design

- 0: random sci-fi shapes and greeble.
- 1: major systems imply function but one source-relevant load, body relation, access/service, heat, biology, interface, or condition remains vague.
- 2: the applicable shape, material, body relation, access/service, condition, power, heat, biology, and interface evidence reveal how the system or place operates without adding unused systems.

When UI is present, a score of 2 also requires a system-specific carrier/signaling medium and topology, source-derived perception/input or governance/system coupling, task hierarchy, independent feedback, applicable degraded logic, and large readable state relations rather than decorative data.

### 7. Body, behavior, and material reality

- 0: plastic/mannequin bodies, cloned groups, impossible contact, behavior that contradicts the environment, or generic materials with no world relation.
- 1: bodies, objects, and materials are plausible but the source-specific attention, relation, use, stillness, history, or environmental response is weak.
- 2: people, organisms, objects, traces, and materials express the selected beat through specific body mechanics, gaze, gesture, relation, contact or absence, localized history, and environment response.

### 8. Source specificity, originality, and requested ambition

- 0: the image repeats a common genre/franchise template, fails the requested imagination amplitude, or uses scale only as an adjective when scale is part of the source claim.
- 1: the result is coherent and has a source-related distinction, but one important world, body, operation, scale, or identity relationship remains generic.
- 2: the dominant function-specific relationship, design system, and all scale evidence the source actually needs are unmistakably specific to this beat and requested amplitude, without borrowing an existing franchise. Two or three independent scale proofs and contemporary-substitution resistance are required only when giant scale or amplitude II–III makes them relevant.

## Conditional extension score

Apply these checks inside the same eight axes; do not create bonus points beyond 16.

- **Giant mecha**: axis 2 requires support, inertia, power/heat and environmental response; axis 6 requires mission-derived frame, actuation, operating/governance/automation relation, and only the source-relevant lifecycle/service or degraded logic; axis 7 requires a readable command/load-transfer or occupied-system relation when the beat uses it; axis 8 requires three scale proofs and an original silhouette.
- **Nonhuman organism/ecology**: axis 2 requires separate story/science status plus consistent habitat, energy/ecological role, exchange, sensing, movement/growth, and environment response; axis 6 requires organization and regional material behavior derived before silhouette; axis 7 requires source-specific attention, response, contact, stillness, or withheld behavior; axis 8 fails when familiar-animal anatomy, engineered-shell grammar, decorative emission, or a transplanted Earth ecology supplies the identity. Giant scale additionally uses the megafauna checks.
- **Megafauna**: axis 2 requires support medium, change/motion limit and environmental response; axis 6 requires organization/body plan derived from habitat and life process; axis 7 requires the applicable material, exchange, energy, sensing, fatigue/dormancy, growth, healing or injury behavior without forcing Earth-animal anatomy; axis 8 requires three scale proofs and no enlarged-familiar-organism template.
- **Ordinary future equipment**: axis 2 requires its actual function and applicable energy/load/environment consequence; axis 6 requires only the interfaces/relationships, energy/data, logistics/lifecycle, service, protection, and state the brief uses; axis 7 requires role/body/environment relation when embodied; axis 8 requires a source-specific, non-franchise system family without inventing combat or modular electronics.
- **Weapon/defense/rescue-hazard equipment**: axis 2 requires effect, reaction/heat and source-relevant consequence; axis 6 requires applicable echelon, governance/authorization, power/data, logistics, service, protection/safe envelope, and current state; axis 7 requires role/body/environment relation when a user is present; axis 8 requires a non-contemporary, non-franchise system family.
- **Scene and world**: axis 1 requires the selected function to need this place and moment; axis 3 requires one connected geography appropriate to the production route; axis 6 requires only the scarcity, institution, logistics, culture, or history that the source makes relevant; axis 7 requires source-appropriate purposeful activity, relation, stillness, or causal emptiness; axis 8 fails if `generic sci-fi base` can replace the location without losing logic.
- **Style and color**: axis 4 requires the selected style thesis to become a camera/viewer relation rather than a label; axis 5 requires a coherent source hierarchy, only the active color responsibilities, visible exposure, and source-relevant continuity/transition; axis 6 requires material color and aging to remain causal; axis 8 requires the palette to belong to this world rather than a familiar grade.
- **Multi-image continuity**: apply every axis to both each original frame and the story-order CONTACT-SHEET. An adjacent frame cannot pass if its incoming/outgoing state, spatial axis, light/color state, material/damage history, or family identity drifts without cause.
- **Future interface in a narrative/world frame**: axis 1 must pass the selected visual contract without reading UI text; axis 2 requires interface and physical/world state to agree; axes 4–6 require source-derived modality/governance, production behavior, task-specific topology, critical confirmation, feedback, and applicable fallback. **Isolated UI/HMI asset**: use the declared task/system model, internal redundant cues, modality, state coverage, and production constraints instead of external physical/world evidence. In either case axis 8 fails if it can be transplanted unchanged to another task/world.

For mixed mecha–organism frames, both subjects must pass their conditional checks. One convincing subject cannot hide the other's failure.

## Blockbuster still benchmark

Use this section only when the user requests a blockbuster-scale live-action film-still target. It is a visual-development benchmark, not a claim of finished feature-film quality. Other selected production routes use the same eight axes without being forced into this look.

Treat the result as multiplicative: `physical credibility × production credibility × design ambition`. A polished research illustration with low design ambition cannot pass as a blockbuster still, and imaginative spectacle with broken physics or no photographed anchor cannot pass either. The score threshold never overrides the imagination-amplitude veto.

- **15–16**: strong cinematic still candidate; ready for user judgment and possible targeted refinement.
- **14**: internal pass; one secondary weakness remains visible.
- **11–13**: competent concept or previsualization image, not yet a convincing film still.
- **8–10**: generic science-fiction concept image.
- **0–7**: failed direction.

Even a 16/16 static image cannot prove performance across motion, editing, sound, continuity, production design, or theatrical projection.

For a high-imagination validation batch, require at least 80% of frames to pass the contemporary-substitution test. If three or more frames can be summarized as “workers beside gray equipment performing maintenance,” reject the batch direction even when individual craft scores are competent.

## Large-scale live-action quality translation

Do not score direct imitation of a named filmmaker. Judge these observable properties instead:

- spectacle and the source's human, nonhuman, relational, informational, or atmospheric anchor coexist in one beat;
- camera relation is physically credible and serves information order, whether close, embedded, remote, fixed, or observational;
- large-format perspective uses a story-derived distance and lens rather than automatic ultra-wide distortion or automatic moderate-lens proximity;
- photographed source-required body, object, material, environment, light, reflection, or intentional-empty-space evidence leads; practical contact is required only when the beat contains contact, and VFX extends rather than contradicts the photographed anchor;
- physical phenomena, light, and color are causally motivated;
- large calm surfaces and selective engineering detail create scale;
- exposure, black level, highlight behavior, texture, and optical artifacts follow the selected capture system, display intent, light sources, and dramatic information order rather than a fixed prestige recipe;
- the scene avoids generic cyberpunk, game-cinematic, and concept-art polish.

## Diagnostic workflow

1. Check vetoes.
2. Score all eight axes from the visible image.
3. Name the single dominant failure that most reduces belief or emotion.
4. Trace it to source authority, function contract, physics, camera, production layer, light, design ambition, body behavior, or world logic.
5. Change the one upstream mechanism, blocking, authority decision, or style/production cause that most directly repairs the failure.
6. Re-score only after inspecting the new image at normal viewing size and close detail.

Do not repair a physics error with extra mood, a story failure with more scale or UI, an interface failure with denser data, or a CG-render failure with more grain.
