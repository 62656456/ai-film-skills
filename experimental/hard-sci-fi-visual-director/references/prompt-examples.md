# Balanced prompt patterns and validation cases

Load this file only when the user explicitly asks for examples or a validation set. Never load it to compensate for a sparse screenplay: build the script authority/gap card and research the actual gaps instead.

These cases test whether the Skill changes its visual reasoning with the screenplay. They are not a house world, approved project bible, scientific citation set, or library of imagery to copy. Do not reuse their characters, environments, palette, camera, equipment, or plot in real work.

## Universal prompt skeleton

```text
[deliverable and ratio]

[current source/version and must-preserve beat]

[selected function-specific visual contract and information order]

[dominant composition and physically/sensor-valid viewer relation]

[script-derived world/environment evidence and only relevant physical consequences]

[body, relationship, object, system, organism, or spatial behavior required by the beat]

[production route and applicable practical/extension layers]

[custom style thesis translated into camera relation, rhythm, material-information distribution, and contrast]

[color/light as source → medium/material → exposure → story role → continuity state]

[UI only if required: task/test model, topology, human/nonhuman/collective/autonomous or no-operator relation, input/governance/feedback, carrier or signaling medium, applicable degraded mode, post-text boundary]

[short scene-specific exclusions]
```

Do not insert fields that the source does not need. An asset reference need not invent a protagonist; a relationship beat need not list a heat budget; an action beat with critical control may need both.

## Validation 1 — Quiet discovery

Raw source:

```text
During a scheduled dark interval in an under-ice nursery, a child wakes and notices an unknown migrating organism beyond the observation layer. The child switches off a personal reading light so the organism approaches, then chooses not to wake anyone. The beat is awe and secret-keeping, not an emergency.
```

Required derivation:

- primary contract: `observer → evidence → attention/understanding shift`, with identity of the organism partly withheld;
- preserve the scheduled darkness, child's deliberate light action, sleeping group, approach, and secrecy;
- do not silently name the world: `under-ice` is FACT, while Europa, Enceladus, Earth, an exoplanet, or an artificial ice habitat remains an explicit PROPOSAL until the user or source locks it;
- load `organism-and-ecology.md`; treat the organism's existence and approach as story FACT while its anatomy, energy path, scale, sensing, color, emission, and ecology remain DERIVATION or PROPOSAL with separate science status;
- research the selected proposal's ice/medium boundary, pressure and thermal separation, observation method, habitat lighting, energy ecology, and plausible organism sensing before design;
- compare at least three organization routes when body identity matters, including one non-centralized route; delete familiar whale/snake/insect, vehicle-shell, franchise-creature, decorative blue/cyan glow, LED-dot, and unsupported tentacle defaults;
- distinguish direct light sensing from correlated heat, electrical, chemical, or schedule cues; switching off a lamp does not by itself prove photoreception or bioluminescence;
- derive color from real emitters, absorption/scatter, material, exposure, and the reveal order;
- choose camera distance and lens from the child's hidden attention, not from a default “operator shoulder” shot;
- for a single still, select one compatible threshold moment and state that the complete `lamp off → approach → chooses secrecy` sequence cannot be fully proven without a durable trace, second frame, or motion;
- no failure, alarm, rescue, sacrifice, handwheel, adult technician, or mandatory UI.

Failure test: if the output becomes a leaking base, warning screen, repair task, creature attack, silently locked Europa scene, giant glowing sea serpent, mechanical organism, or over-compressed three-action still, the Skill rewrote or overfilled the source.

## Validation 2 — Relationship and power

Raw source:

```text
At the end of a public meal in a rotating habitat, a grandmother quietly moves her family's single ceremonial seat token from her own wrist to her granddaughter's travel bag after learning the girl plans to surrender her place. Neither speaks. Other diners continue an ordinary evening.
```

Required derivation:

- primary contract: `object exchange and access control → response → relational change`;
- derive spatial gravity and public/private visibility from the habitat and scene, but keep the token exchange dominant;
- let background life continue only to the degree it reinforces the private act inside public space;
- style, palette, and camera emerge from intimacy, concealment, rotating-world geometry, and approved production route;
- no invented mechanical breakdown, confrontation, countdown, or operations display.

Failure test: if engineering operation becomes more legible than the relationship, the output fails even when physics is correct.

## Validation 3 — Establishing and atmosphere

Raw source:

```text
Before sunrise, a settlement built inside the slowly moving boundary between permanent shadow and reflected light begins its daily school route. No protagonist and no accident; the shot should teach the viewer how children, transport, heat, and public space adapt to the moving boundary.
```

Required derivation:

- primary contract: `location function → active world → latent environmental condition/route`;
- research the selected world's illumination and thermal conditions before choosing architecture or palette;
- build connected geography and causal daily behavior, not a collection of landmark buildings;
- use children only because the source contains them; background actions have routes and purposes;
- no mandatory state failure or heroic machine.

Failure test: if the frame could be relabeled as a generic gray space colony, the world-to-scene chain is too weak.

## Validation 4 — Aftermath and withheld cause

Raw source:

```text
A scientific vessel returns to a floating settlement with every external surface clean except for a perfectly sharp band where living material has begun to grow. The crew are absent from the frame. The audience should infer contact, but not yet know whether it was contamination, repair, or invitation.
```

Required derivation:

- primary contract: `material trace → implied cause → changed social/environmental state`;
- preserve ambiguity among the three interpretations;
- derive material boundary, cleaning history, water/atmosphere behavior, docking geography, and observer distance;
- no explanatory UI, hazard team, obvious monster, quarantine text, or alarm unless the current script later adds them.

Failure test: if the image reveals a single definite cause, it violates information order.

## Validation 5 — Action with consequential interface

Raw source:

```text
A pilot must transfer a damaged vehicle's remaining thermal capacity from navigation to the occupied refuge module before a debris pass. The physical coolant branch, the pilot's guarded control, and the refuge module's changing radiator state are all in view. Failure of navigation is the accepted cost.
```

Required derivation:

- primary contract: `pilot → guarded transfer → physical branch → refuge state change → navigation loss`;
- pass the immediate no-text causal gate before interface design;
- research relevant human-factors, thermal-control, physical feedback, and capture evidence;
- interface follows real topology/orientation, supports one decision, has independent confirmation, and cannot be a black rectangle with a percentage;
- critical state appears in both the interface/control and the physical branch/radiator/module response.

Failure test: if the story only works after reading tiny screen text, reblock the scene.

## Validation 6 — Isolated equipment family

Raw source:

```text
Create a neutral reference image for a standardized expedition equipment family used by three body types in a corrosive high-pressure atmosphere. Show shared interfaces, scale, protective logic, role-specific modules, and service access. No user, battle, damage event, or narrative crisis is requested.
```

Required derivation:

- primary contract: `family identity → function → three body scales → common interfaces → material/protection/service logic`;
- research environment, body reach/accommodation, corrosion, sealing, maintenance, and logistics before form;
- use a diagnostic production/reference route rather than pretending this is a film still;
- no adult hero, enemy, battle pose, collapsed module, or dramatic warning UI.

Failure test: if the output invents a firefight or makes three cosmetic variants of one contemporary object, it fails.

## Validation 7 — High-imagination world identity

Raw source:

```text
An orbital city has no permanent streets. Once each local month, its inhabited membranes exchange curvature and the population moves through temporary rooms that become public routes for six minutes. Show the first exchange witnessed by a newcomer; it is wondrous and socially awkward, not catastrophic.
```

Required derivation:

- primary contract is discovery; establishing is an explicit secondary contract that must support rather than replace the newcomer's wonder and awkwardness, with no invented failure;
- amplitude II or III may be appropriate only after a verified anchor, explicit hypothesis, costs, and downstream consequences are recorded;
- compare at least three topology/behavior routes and reject source/familiar franchise form;
- derive bodies, belongings, access, institutions, light, camera, and production layers from changing curvature;
- present-day substitution must break the beat after names, UI, grade, and location labels are removed.

Failure test: a familiar corridor bending with extra holograms is not a world-system transformation.

## Validation 8 — Same story, different user locks

Apply the same approved beat twice:

```text
Route A lock: photographed observational drama, distant public viewpoint, natural environmental illumination.
Route B lock: tactile miniature-led expression, close subjective viewpoint, explicitly stylized but causal material color.
```

Both outputs must preserve identical plot facts, outcome, relationship, and information order while legitimately changing production route, camera relation, material treatment, contrast, and palette. If the story changes with the style, authority failed. If the images look the same despite the locks, derivation failed.

## Validation report format

For each case report:

- source facts preserved;
- invented facts or prohibited rewrite, if any;
- selected function contract and whether it passes without UI/text;
- research questions formed before design;
- world/environment/style/color/camera decisions and their authority/status;
- default leakage: crisis, maintenance, adult technician, handwheel, gray equipment, amber task light, moderate lens, single-source light, or generic black UI;
- final result: PASS, WEAK, or FAIL with one dominant cause.
