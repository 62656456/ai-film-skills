# Giant mecha and megafauna route

Load this reference only when the requested image contains a giant operated machine, a city-scale organism, or their interaction. Preserve source authority, physics, production design, camera, and aesthetic gates; apply live-action rules only when that production route is selected.

## Evidence boundary

- Treat a very large free-walking humanoid machine or land animal as an **explicit hypothesis**, not verified future engineering.
- Translate franchise references into general mechanisms only: relative scale, load/support, body or operator relation, access, mass transfer, environmental response, and viewpoint consequence. Choose which mechanisms matter from the current script; never copy named machines, creatures, silhouettes, colors, cockpit layouts, weapons, marks, or signature compositions.
- Prefer a small number of explicit physical leaps, not a visually small result. Mission-specific heavy frames, distributed-contact machines, tethered or externally powered systems, partly buoyant organisms, low-gravity ecologies, coastal shallows, and support from terrain or infrastructure may grow into city- or civilization-scale relationships when their costs remain visible.

## Giant-system imaginative scope

Before the support card, write one world-exclusive visual thesis. A giant subject should not merely be a current excavator, crane, animal, or armored person enlarged; its body or operation must create a source-relevant change in infrastructure, movement, work, ecology, habitation, perception, culture, or safety.

For amplitude II–III:

- combine one verified load/energy/biological invariant, one non-adjacent operating principle, and one explicit discontinuity;
- generate three functionally valid body/topology candidates with different support geometry, occupied relationship, and spatial role before locking a silhouette;
- propagate the selected body plan into at least three visible consequences across city/terrain structure, operator or organism behavior, maintenance/feeding, institution/economy, evacuation/safety, or lifecycle;
- for a narrative frame, place the source-required bodies, infrastructure, and environmental response in one function-specific relationship rather than a side-by-side scale comparison; for an isolated asset reference, show function, support, applicable interfaces/relationships, and scale without inventing action;
- run the contemporary-substitution test: if an ordinary crane, ship, tracked vehicle, familiar animal, or conventional giant humanoid can replace the subject without changing the event, redesign it.

Spectacle may come from the giant becoming infrastructure, changing a landscape, carrying habitation, altering ecology, reorganizing ritual or labor, creating an exclusion zone, or producing another script-derived relation. Sacrifice, damage, and emergency are optional. Keep decisive contact physically believable and let the full consequence extend beyond the practical set when the selected production route supports it.

## Shared giant-scale card

Record before styling:

1. **Height and mass class**: use qualitative bands unless the story supplies values. Separate visible height from implied mass.
2. **Support medium**: ground, shallow water, full buoyancy, low gravity, ice, soft sediment, structural deck, or mixed boundary.
3. **Support/contact relation**: only the applicable discrete supports, distributed contact, anchoring, suspension, buoyancy, aerodynamic support, field coupling, colony relation, growth substrate, or other explicit mechanism.
4. **Mass/support transfer**: trace force, pressure, buoyancy, tension, compression, distributed flow, or another physical relation through the actual machine topology or organism organization; do not assume limbs, pelvis, keel, torso, or payload.
5. **Motion/change envelope**: applicable translation, rotation, deformation, flow, growth, colony reconfiguration, acceleration, turning/reorientation, response time, and exclusion/consequence zone. Large scale constrains change unless an explicit mechanism pays the cost.
6. **Machine energy or biological economy**: for machines, applicable source/storage/distribution/peak/heat/safe state; for organisms, applicable energy uptake, metabolism or non-terrestrial analogue, exchange/transport, homeostasis, heat/material release, dormancy, or recovery. Do not give a creature a battery/tether or a machine a metabolism unless the premise is hybrid.
7. **Scale proofs**: use at least three appropriate independent relationships. Include an occupied interface only when occupation is source-relevant and readable; remote, autonomous, unoccupied, organism, colony, or structure cases may use bodies, habitat features, repeated organization, displacement, travel time, footprint, terrain, buildings, vehicles, or other known relations without inventing occupants.

Reject a city-scale body whose tiny feet, crouched joints, instant acceleration, silent landing, or weightless collision contradict its mass.

## Giant mecha system

Derive every major shape from the source-required mission or social role: lifting, construction, transport, habitation, cultivation, ritual, exploration, containment, rescue, defense, assembly, hazard interception, or another named function.

### Structure and actuation

- Derive frame topology, contact geometry, surface articulation, joint exposure, redundancy, and service strategy from mission, medium, gravity, scale, manufacturing, operating life, and the explicit speculative rule. External frames, broad pads, calm skins, accessible joints, and redundant locks are options, not a house silhouette.
- Concentrate detail at the chosen topology's actual actuation, support, energy/heat, sensing, access, protection, manufacture, contact, or history zones. Bearings, armor overlaps, impact zones, and repair patches appear only when the mechanism and current state use them; keep other load-bearing or functional surfaces at a source-derived detail density.
- Derive a named actuation family from mission, scale, medium, energy, maintenance, and the explicit world rule. Electro-hydraulic, cable, screw, linear-motor, biological, field-coupled, or another mechanism may be valid; speculative actuation needs an explicit hypothesis and observable cost rather than decorative glow.
- Show only the support and control mechanisms the chosen topology requires: joint or structural locks, stance or buoyancy control, counter-torque, compliant contact, prepared ground/fluid coupling, access, or another explicit solution. Any massive body must establish a believable load path before exerting force; it need not be bipedal or use feet.
- If the source shows damage, give it one specific loss and causal evidence such as a leaking working fluid, jammed bearing, severed sensor cluster, overheated bus, cracked protection over an intact frame, failed lock, or isolated actuator. Do not add random full-body sparks.

### Operating, control, and occupied relation

- Derive crew count and roles from scale, mission, latency, authority, body accommodation, automation, and safety. A single operator is valid when the system's complexity, automation, and degraded fallback support it; multiple roles are not mandatory by genre.
- Do not default to instantaneous one-to-one human motion mapping for a city-scale machine. Manual, gaze, voice, neural, embodied, distributed, nonhuman, predictive, or autonomous control may be valid; if the source uses full-body mapping, show how latency, amplification, filtering, support, safety, and machine mass prevent impossible mirroring.
- When the beat contains a command or motion transition, show latency and mass through the applicable command/autonomous decision, load transfer, structural/medium response, and resulting motion. A quiet observation or neutral asset may show a stable support/energy state without inventing a command or movement.
- When operators exist, derive their protected, open, remote, distributed, or embodied relation from environment and mission. Show restraint, access, escape, isolation, and degraded mode only to the degree mission and hazard require them.
- Put interface information on a body-, environment-, task-, and medium-derived carrier. Human hands and gaze, nonhuman senses, collective signaling, remote supervision, or autonomous state feedback must connect to the system change only when that operating relation exists in the source.

### Movement and interaction logic

- When the beat contains movement, stage one legible mass-transfer phase such as plant, brace, turn, carry, kneel, reach, push, recover, or controlled fall. A quiet observation or asset reference may show stable support instead of action.
- Let inertia propagate through whatever the chosen body and medium actually contain: delayed shell or tissue motion, tension change, support compression, fluid displacement, ground response, wake, counter-motion, or structural vibration are conditional examples rather than a required bundle.
- Reject acrobatics, high kicks, superhero landings, weightless punches, instant reversals, or symmetric face-off poses when their mass, support, energy, momentum, and terrain costs are missing. An explicit world rule may permit extreme movement only when its mechanism and visible consequences pay those costs.
- For conflict, compare redirection, containment, leverage, terrain use, evacuation cover, infrastructure protection, ranged standoff, disengagement, or direct impact according to the script; do not default to repeated weightless fist trading.
- If a weapon or hazardous effect system is present, route it through the weapon/defense/rescue-hazard gate and show only the effect's applicable support/reaction, energy/heat, lifecycle or consumables, access/governance, safety envelope, and collateral relation. A stowed, passive, one-shot, non-consumable, or neutral reference must not be forced into active firing evidence.

## Giant organism system

Derive anatomy from habitat and life process before silhouette.

### Biological support

- Large terrestrial organisms generally need straighter load-bearing limbs, high duty factor, broad feet, reduced acceleration, and conservative turning. Very large crouched bipeds require a stronger explicit hypothesis.
- Use water, dense atmosphere, lower gravity, distributed limbs, buoyancy chambers, compliant feet, or partial body support when the premise needs greater size.
- Define only the organism's applicable support/organization, locomotion or growth, gas/liquid/chemical/material exchange, internal or distributed transport, homeostasis/heat, sensing, energy uptake, reproduction/lifecycle, and recovery/dormancy. Tissues, joints, lungs, circulation, feeding, and a centralized body are conditional Earth-derived implementations, not universal biology.
- Make repeated organization functional rather than decorative. Cells, modules, branches, pressure chambers, fibers, tissues, armor, spines, sacs, plates, membranes, tendons, fields, symbionts, or bioluminescence require a habitat or life-process role when used.
- Do not merely enlarge a familiar reptile, ape, insect, shark, or dinosaur. Combine environment-derived functions into an original body plan.

### Creature movement and material reality

- Before visible displacement, show the propulsion/support mechanism's own preparatory state: muscle/tissue loading, pressure redistribution, ciliary or membrane phase, buoyancy change, fluid jet, colony synchronization, growth release, environmental coupling, or another explicit process. Do not assume a trunk, limb, tendon, step, or strike.
- A fall is catastrophic at great scale. Giant organisms avoid casual jumping and rapid direction changes unless buoyancy or low gravity clearly supports them.
- Breathing or other exchange, condensation, heat/color change, mucus/brine, dust, injury fluid, symbionts, parasites, shedding, or material release appear only where the medium, organism model, and current state permit.
- The body/environment boundary must show regional function where the organism has regions: load-bearing or buoyant surface, flexible boundary, sensory field, healing zone, exchange patch, salt crust, pressure adaptation, colony edge, or another mechanism. Reject uniform rubber skin and equal-detail decoration, but do not force skin, scars, joints, or vascular tissue.
- Environmental response must follow the event: water displacement, ground settlement, localized debris, flexing structures, wake, or pressure change. Do not use a generic explosion cloud as the scale proof.

## Mecha–organism interaction card

For a shared frame, record:

1. each body's objective, attention, or stillness, plus route only when it is moving;
2. the relevant contact/near-contact point, separation, sightline, signal path, or shared boundary;
3. support state before and after only when the beat changes it;
4. force path only when load or contact occurs; otherwise record the information, attention, signal, habitat, or environmental relation;
5. the source-relevant person, organism, habitat, infrastructure, territory, institution, or environmental constraint;
6. the selected action, discovery, relationship, establishing, aftermath, or reference evidence that makes the frame more than a size comparison.

Use one dominant interaction derived from the screenplay. It may be conflict, rescue, labor, migration, communication, care, observation, ritual, avoidance, or coexistence; do not default to a charge, evacuation, barrier, or grapple.

## Camera, light, and live-action layers

- Derive camera position from the selected dramatic function, information order, scale relation, and production route. A rescue vehicle, domestic window, cockpit, harbor deck, remote observatory, ground path, sensor mast, waterline, or nonhuman viewpoint may each be valid.
- Derive proximity, occlusion, and completeness from the selected function. Crop the giant when off-frame continuation strengthens scale or subjectivity; show a complete body when geography, comparison, ritual, confrontation, or asset diagnosis requires it.
- Keep the source-required human or nonhuman emotional/perceptual anchor readable when it matters: face, gaze, hand, body posture, occupied interface, group relation, sensory organ, or intentional absence. Do not insert an adult or crew solely for scale.
- For photographed live-action/hybrid routes, choose a practical contact/performance layer the beat truly contains—cockpit, body or skin contact, room, street, deck, vehicle, landscape, tool, waterline, or another anchor—and extend only what production cannot capture. For animation, illustration, miniature, sensor, or asset-reference routes, derive an equivalent coherent layer strategy without forcing a full-scale foreground or damage/rescue prop.
- Use environmental or operational light: daylight, storm-scattered sky, fire only in an allowed atmosphere, sodium/white work lamps, cockpit task lights, or lightning with a readable direction. Avoid universal blue-orange edge lighting.

## Route vetoes

Reject the image or prompt if any applicable item appears:

- for narrative frames, an unsupported centered full-body machine, chest-light identity, hero stance, or showroom composition that erases the selected beat; an explicit source-motivated confrontation, ceremony, inspection, portrait, or asset reference may use frontal or complete geometry when it remains functionally and dramatically diagnostic;
- copied franchise silhouette, color blocking, cockpit, creature head, weapon, insignia, or face-off composition;
- city-scale body with contact, posture, acceleration, turn, impact, or aerial motion that its support medium, gravity, actuation, energy, momentum control, and explicit speculative rule cannot pay for;
- no power source, no heat path, no source-relevant service/degradation strategy or required operating-system protection, or decorative greeble everywhere;
- cockpit crew moving like dancers while the machine mirrors them instantly;
- giant organism that is a familiar animal enlarged without support, respiration, circulation, heat, habitat, or locomotion consequences;
- rubber skin, random glowing veins, ornamental spikes, identical scales, or roar-at-camera as the only event;
- explosion, rain, fog, dust, spray, or fire that contradicts the medium;
- size proved only by a wide lens, tiny people, skyscraper comparison, or adjectives;
- combat with no source-relevant affected body/space, terrain or medium, energy/load, safety envelope, or collateral logic; civilians are required only when the screenplay includes them.
- amplitude II–III that remains a walking crane, enlarged familiar animal, ordinary vehicle with legs, or conventional armored humanoid after labels, setting, and color are removed.
