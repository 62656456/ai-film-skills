# Original humanoid combat-mecha form direction

Load this reference only when combat, armed defense, interception, deterrence, battlefield survival, or military responsibility defines a mecha's identity. Load it after `mecha-and-megafauna.md`; also load `future-weapon-systems.md` only when a weapon or hazardous effect is visible or design-changing.

This module owns the mecha's visual proposition, silhouette, humanoid body rhythm, author identity, combat readability, armor/inner-frame rhythm, human-scale relation, and cinematic presentation. `mecha-and-megafauna.md` owns mass, support, actuation, operation, and environment response. `future-weapon-systems.md` owns effect, authorization, safety, logistics, and the non-actionable boundary.

## Primary outcome and precedence

The goal is not the greatest number of justified mechanisms. The goal is an attractive, memorable, original humanoid combat machine whose engineering strengthens its form.

Use this order and stop at the first failed stage:

`visual proposition → black silhouette → body rhythm and proportion → two or three identity anchors → combat responsibility in the body → scale relation → engineering resolution → surface/material state → cinematic scene`

- Engineering may veto an impossible form or explain a strong form. It may not replace the visual proposition with a systems checklist.
- Story, mechanics, references, and surface detail must converge on one body grammar. Do not let each source own a different body region.
- A technically coherent but visually generic, awkward, or forgettable body fails before prompt writing.
- More armor fragments, exposed cylinders, weapons, lights, or greeble do not increase authorship.

## Single-author and design-repair mode

Enter `SINGLE_AUTHOR_REPAIR` when the user asks for one designer/no cross-design, rejects a result as uglier or regressive, or asks to repair the machine rather than expand the project.

In this mode:

1. The primary agent alone owns aesthetic diagnosis, form selection, and the final design language. Do not delegate visual ideation, merge parallel concepts, or average review opinions into the body.
2. Freeze every element the user still accepts—such as story, atmosphere, camera feeling, scale relation, or color mood—and name the single visible form failure. Do not reopen accepted areas to make the process look comprehensive.
3. A rejected image is evidence of failure, never a shape anchor. Preserve only the traits the user explicitly kept.
4. Do not expand the reference pool or generate a route matrix unless one missing fact genuinely blocks the correction. Existing references may test the chosen route; they may not contribute parts.
5. Commit to one coherent replacement. Private thumbnail thinking is allowed, but do not expose several options, combine them, or ask the user to curate them unless the user explicitly requests alternatives.
6. Repair the machine before the scene. Do not use lighting, damage, crowds, weapons, atmosphere, or cinematic crop to disguise an unresolved body.
7. Do not generate another image until the user authorizes generation after the form direction is presented, unless the user's latest instruction explicitly requests immediate generation.

## Visual proposition and silhouette lock

Before a system card, reference matrix, or detailed engineering description, write a one- or two-sentence visual proposition without brand names and without a list of components. It must state:

`first-read character and emotional posture → dominant body relationship or negative space → why this combat role needs that body`

Then perform a black-silhouette test at small viewing size:

- the head/sensor carrier, shoulder field, chest, waist, pelvis, two arms, and two legs form a readable humanoid chain;
- one dominant mass and one counter-mass, void, offset, or directional gesture create a memorable whole;
- the body has deliberate compression and release rather than equal thickness everywhere;
- the center of gravity and support are believable without producing a crouched ape, construction walker, or tank on legs;
- the outline remains identifiable without weapon, color, mark, light, damage, background, or pose spectacle;
- the silhouette can be summarized from memory in one short sentence after a brief look.

If the silhouette cannot pass, do not add hydraulics, lore, weather, or a more dramatic camera. Redesign the silhouette.

## Body rhythm and identity anchors

Resolve the whole body before local parts:

- establish the proportion and tension among head, shoulder field, ribcage/chest, torque waist, pelvis, thigh, knee, lower leg, foot, upper arm, forearm, and hand;
- make the pelvis and waist intentional structural forms rather than hidden gaps between a box chest and legs;
- decide where the body is calm, where it changes direction, and where detail concentrates;
- use asymmetry only when it strengthens role, movement, damage, or character; random left/right difference is noise;
- choose only two or three identity anchors. Each anchor must alter silhouette, negative space, body rhythm, or movement—not merely decorate a panel;
- make the anchors cooperate as one visual sentence. If they compete for attention, delete the weakest one.

The head cannot carry the entire identity. The chest/waist/pelvis rhythm and at least one limb or second-silhouette relation must also be distinctive.

## Reference abstraction without collage

References are critique lenses, not body-part donors.

1. Write the current design thesis before naming references.
2. For each relevant source, extract only one upper-level lesson such as proportion readability, assembly hierarchy, equipment order, mass behavior, human touch, or environmental interaction.
3. Ask whether that lesson strengthens or challenges the same thesis. If it pushes toward a second unrelated visual language, reject it rather than blend it.
4. Never assign one source to the head, another to armor, another to joints, another to weight, and another to color.
5. Delete recognizable face, crest, chest, shoulder, backpack, weapon, palette, marking, pose, and signature composition before prompt writing.

Named sources may remain in internal research notes. The image prompt must be brand-free and describe only observable relationships.

## Request relationship and continuity

Classify only when it changes the work:

- `SINGLE_FLAGSHIP`: one new or selected combat mecha;
- `SAME_WORLD_FAMILY`: related models sharing an approved manufacturing and author grammar;
- `SAME_MECHA_VARIANTS`: one locked machine in causally different states or missions;
- `INDEPENDENT_STORY_SET`: separate machines only when the user or separate authorities actually require them.

Different scenes or stories do not automatically require different machines. Similarity is valid when continuity, faction, function, or convergence explains it; difference is valid when responsibility, world, scale, support, or environment genuinely changes the body. Image count alone decides nothing.

When the user requests one design, do not create a multi-route deliverable. When a body is approved, preserve its silhouette, proportion, identity anchors, hardpoints, access, and material grammar unless a named causal change requires revision.

## Human–mecha scale relation

Scale is a story and body-design variable, not a tiny-person decoration. Record only what the current task needs:

`machine scale class | human/operator relation | access or escape | protected/threatened space | intermediate anchors | tactical/emotional consequence | camera distance/height/crop`

- Choose scale from vulnerability, agency, touch, danger radius, mission geometry, environment, and intended emotion—not from a universal height.
- For a new design-identity frame, prefer one purposeful human relation plus the minimum useful intermediate anchor. Do not add a crowd merely to prove size.
- Keep the person and machine in a coherent spatial depth. Do not shrink the person through extreme perspective and call it scale.
- Large scale may need doors, vehicles, platforms, decks, buildings, vegetation, contact deformation, or travel distance to connect the relation.
- Scale evidence may never crop or hide every identity anchor when the frame's purpose is to judge the design.

## Combat responsibility in the body

After the silhouette and body rhythm pass, make the source-defined responsibility visible without relying on removable weapons.

Interception, breakthrough, escort, suppression, protection, reconnaissance, denial, or another role may alter stance range, shoulder field, torso orientation, pelvis and support, sensing, protection zoning, limb leverage, second silhouette, heat/recovery state, payload access, or occupied space. Use only the relations the story needs.

Run `weapon-hidden`: remove handheld and detachable equipment. If the remaining body could be any generic armored humanoid, revise one foundational body relationship rather than adding a larger weapon.

## Engineering resolution after form lock

Choose one coherent actuation and construction family after the body is visually locked. Resolve only the visible mechanisms needed to make that body move, stop, carry load, survive, cool, admit or protect its operator, and enter the current state.

- trace the main frame, bearings, torque/load path, support, lock, and recovery state;
- let armor open, slide, overlap, or separate only where motion and service require it;
- concentrate complete mechanisms at shoulder, waist/pelvis, hip, knee, ankle, wrist, heat, access, or payload nodes actually visible in the chosen view;
- keep broad armor or structural masses calm enough to preserve the silhouette;
- expose a cylinder, linkage, hose, cable, manifold, gear, or bearing only when both its connection and purpose can be inferred;
- do not make every joint hydraulic, every gap exposed, or every surface equally detailed;
- damage follows one specific failure and current load state rather than random full-body ruin.

Use `mecha-and-megafauna.md` for detailed physical checks. Do not duplicate its whole engineering checklist in the visual-design output.

## Design-identity frame and cinematic story frame

Do not force one image to perform two incompatible jobs.

### `DESIGN_IDENTITY_FRAME`

Use first when a new design is unapproved, a prior image hid the body, or the user is specifically judging appearance.

- show enough of the head/sensor relation, chest/waist/pelvis rhythm, limbs, support, and second silhouette to judge the body;
- use a quiet real environment or restrained photographic stage, coherent natural/functional light, and one scale relation when relevant;
- allow cinematic perspective and partial occlusion, but not a centered toy-box render, blank showroom, dramatic darkness, or crop that hides the design;
- keep story clutter, crowds, weapons, smoke, sparks, weather spectacle, and heavy damage subordinate.

### `CINEMATIC_STORY_FRAME`

Use after identity is approved or when the user explicitly prioritizes a story image. Derive camera, crop, occlusion, action, light, environment response, and human behavior from the selected beat. Preserve the approved body instead of redesigning it inside the scene.

If both are needed, deliver separate frames derived from one contract; do not compromise both in a single image.

## Multi-mecha work

Only for a genuinely plural family or independent set, make each body answer its own story responsibility, scale relation, support, environment, movement, and emotional function. Do not create divergence merely by changing armor, weapon, backpack, damage, color, weather, or background. For `SAME_MECHA_VARIANTS`, continuity is the goal, not maximal difference.

Run:

- `weapon-hidden`: identity survives removable equipment;
- `skin-hidden`: identity survives color, marks, text, effects, and setting;
- `sample-leak`: an earlier example group has not become a fixed count, scene menu, body, palette, pose, or composition.

Do not generate a divergence matrix for a single design or single-author repair.

## Stage-appropriate output

For an open or rejected combat-mecha identity, return only what helps the user judge the body:

1. `视觉命题`;
2. `轮廓与身体节奏`;
3. `两到三个身份锚点`;
4. `人机尺度与关系` when applicable;
5. `工程如何解释而不改写造型`;
6. `当前状态与下一验收点`.

Do not automatically output a research encyclopedia, reference-role matrix, multi-route table, full system card, scene card, prompt, or image.

After the design identity is approved—or when the user's latest instruction explicitly requests a direct image—add only the system, reference-deletion, scene/light, prompt, negative, and continuity fields needed for that deliverable. Record a stable `contract_id`; generation and internal pass never equal user approval.

## Vetoes

Reject or redesign when any applicable condition appears:

- the first read is a generic industrial walker, crouched ape, construction rig, tank on legs, or ordinary armored humanoid;
- the visual proposition can only be restated as mission, physics, or engineering fields;
- references have been assigned to different body regions and blended into a collage;
- the silhouette needs weapon, color, light, pose, damage, or background to become identifiable;
- the head is distinctive but chest, waist, pelvis, and limbs remain generic;
- equal-width body segments, box chest, missing waist/pelvis rhythm, oversized forearms, or all-over exposed machinery collapse the humanoid character without a story reason;
- uniform armor, chrome, greeble, panel lines, hydraulics, or glow erases the calm/dense rhythm;
- a design-repair task expands into more references, more routes, more scenes, or another image before fixing the named body failure;
- a single-author request delegates or merges aesthetic design;
- human scale is proved only by tiny passive people, extreme wide-angle distortion, or disconnected background objects;
- a design-validation frame hides the declared identity anchors or becomes a model-box/showroom image;
- light, smoke, rain, sparks, destruction, or camera spectacle is used to sell an unresolved design;
- recognizable franchise face, crest, chest, shoulder, backpack, weapon, palette, mark, pose, or signature composition survives source-form deletion;
- actuation, mass, support, heat, operator relation, or environment response contradicts the selected body;
- actionable weapon construction or manufacturing details are exposed;
- prompt compliance, generation, internal score, or delivery is called user approval.
