## Short English launch post

AI storyboards often name the event but hide the camera plan.

Open Film Skills v1.3.0 is a public toolkit of 19 standalone Agent Skills for script writing, directing, reusable assets, cinematic storyboards, prompts, production, and review. Storyboard Director 5.4.4 now makes every segment heading expose the lens, camera position, path, speed, focus handoff, and physical endpoint.
